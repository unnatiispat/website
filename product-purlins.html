Overview

Cold-formed C and Z purlins for roof and wall framing in industrial sheds, warehouses and pre-engineered buildings. Purlins are specified by profile, depth, flange, lip and thickness.

Typical sizes & grades

  • ProfilesC and Z sections, as required
  • FinishBlack or galvanised, as offered by the manufacturer
  • LengthCut to the lengths ordered, subject to the manufacturer

Availability of each size and grade is confirmed at the time of enquiry.

Applicable standard

—

The applicable standard depends on the grade and product form ordered. We confirm it against the manufacturer’s specification at the time of enquiry.

Dimensions needed to identify weight

  • Profile (C or Z)
  • Web depth × flange × lip (mm)
  • Thickness (mm)
  • Length (m)

Units used

  • kg/m
  • kg/piece

Purlin weight depends on the exact profile; use the manufacturer’s kg/m.

Weight note: Nominal manufacturer weight, theoretical calculated weight and actual dispatch weight are different and can vary within applicable tolerances.
