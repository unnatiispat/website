Steel Weight Reference Centre

How the calculation works

Formulas used

All formulas use a steel density of 7,850 kg/m³. Inputs are in millimetres, lengths in metres.

Round bar / TMT

d = diameter in mm.

kg/m = d² ÷ 162

Flat

w = width, t = thickness, both in mm.

kg/m = w × t × 0.00785

Plate / sheet

L, W and t in mm. Per square metre: kg/m² = t × 7.85.

kg = L × W × t × 0.00785 ÷ 1000

Circular pipe

OD = outside diameter, t = wall thickness, in mm.

kg/m = (OD − t) × t × 0.02466

Square / rectangular hollow

A, B = sides, t = wall thickness, in mm. Corner radii are ignored.

kg/m = 2 × (A + B − 2t) × t × 0.00785

Beam / channel / angle

Take kg/m from the section table or manufacturer catalogue.

kg = kg/m × length × quantity

Theoretical estimate: Nominal manufacturer weight, theoretical calculated weight and actual dispatch weight are different and can vary within applicable tolerances.
