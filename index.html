Why Unnati Ispat

Built for dependable supply, from godown to site

Since 2000 we have focused on the practical side of steel trading: understanding each requirement, sourcing the right material and getting it to you as agreed.

Large godown with crane facilities

Material is stored and handled at our godown, equipped with cranes for loading and unloading heavy sections, coils and bundles.

Experienced material-handling team

A trained team handles, sorts and loads steel safely, so that orders are dispatched in the right size, grade and quantity.

Material inspection before dispatch

Material is checked against the confirmed order (product, size, grade and quantity) before it leaves the godown.

Ready fleet of vehicles for delivery

A ready fleet of vehicles supports timely delivery to customer sites and works, as agreed at the time of order.

Estimate steel weight before you enquire

Work out the theoretical weight of bars, flats, plates, pipes and hollow sections from their dimensions, per metre, per piece and in tonnes.

Steel Weight Reference Centre
  • Round bar / TMTkg/m = d² ÷ 162
  • Flatkg/m = w × t × 0.00785
  • Plate / sheetkg/m² = t × 7.85
  • Circular pipekg/m = (OD − t) × t × 0.02466
