M.S. Annealed Binding Wires

Enhance your Annealing, Pickling, Heat Treatment process and for construction projects with our high-quality M.S. Annealed Binding Wires!

Key Features

  • Material: Mild Steel (M.S.)
  • Annealed for superior flexibility and durability.
  • Ideal for binding and securing construction materials.

Uses

  • Material Binding: Annealing, Pickling, Heat Treatment process.
  • Reinforcement Binding: Ensure the structural integrity of your buildings by securely binding reinforcement bars.
  • Construction Sites: Perfect for various applications in construction, providing a reliable solution for bundling and tying materials.
  • Fencing: Create sturdy fences by securely tying wire mesh or panels.
  • Art and Craft: Explore creative possibilities in art and craft projects, thanks to the flexibility of our annealed wires.

Why Choose our #UnnatiWiron M.S. Annealed Binding Wires?

  • Quality Assurance: Manufactured to meet industry standards, ensuring reliability.
  • Flexibility: Annealed for easy handling, bending, and tying.
  • Durable: Built to withstand the rigors of construction environments.
