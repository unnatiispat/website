Features

Annealed for the way wire is actually used

Annealing softens the wire so that it bends and twists cleanly by hand or tool, holds its tie, and stands up to everyday handling.

  • Annealed for flexibility and easy handling.
  • Made for bending, tying and securing.
  • Durable for construction-site handling.
