Overview

Cold-rolled (C.R.) sheets and coils have a smoother surface and closer thickness control than hot-rolled products. They are used for panels, furniture, enclosures, appliances and pressed components.

Typical sizes & grades

  • Thickness and widthAs per the mill range. Please confirm at enquiry
  • FormCoils or sheets
  • GradesIS 513 grades or as specified

Availability of each size and grade is confirmed at the time of enquiry.

Applicable standard

IS 513

Specification for cold reduced carbon steel sheet and strip.

Dimensions needed to identify weight

  • Thickness (mm)
  • Width (mm)
  • Length (mm) for sheets, or coil weight
  • Quantity

Units used

  • kg/m²
  • kg/sheet
  • tonnes per coil

kg/m² = thickness (mm) × 7.85.
