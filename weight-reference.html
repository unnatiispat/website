Engineering Technical Handbook

Formulas of Weight Calculation

Standard engineering weight estimation formulas for carbon steel, chequered pattern plates, stainless steel, non-ferrous metals (aluminium, copper, brass, lead), and Barlow’s pipe pressure calculation.

Carbon Steel

Carbon Steel Pipes & Tubes

(OD − WT) × WT × 0.02466 = kg/m
  • OD: Outside Diameter in millimetres (mm)
  • WT: Wall Thickness in millimetres (mm)
  • Factor: Density based on 7,850 kg/m³ (π × 7.85 ÷ 1,000)
Quick Example: 48.3 mm OD × 3.2 mm WT pipe:
(48.3 − 3.2) × 3.2 × 0.02466 = 3.559 kg/m
Carbon Steel

Carbon Steel Sheets & Plates

L(m) × W(m) × Thk(mm) × 7.85 = kg/sheet
  • L: Length of plate/sheet in metres (m)
  • W: Width of plate/sheet in metres (m)
  • Thk: Plate thickness in millimetres (mm)
  • Basis: 1 mm plate = 7.850 kg/m²
Quick Example: 2.5 m × 1.25 m × 6 mm plate:
2.5 × 1.25 × 6 × 7.85 = 147.19 kg/plate
Carbon Steel

Chequered (Diamond) Plates

Base Wt + [L(m) × W(m) × 2.1] = Total kg/plate
  • Extra pattern weight: L(m) × W(m) × 2.1 = kg/plate
  • Base plain weight: L(m) × W(m) × Thk(mm) × 7.85
  • Total weight: Base Weight + Extra Weight
Worked Example: 2400 × 1220 × 6 mm Plate Extra pattern: 2.4 × 1.22 × 2.1 = 6.15 kg
Base weight: 2.4 × 1.22 × 6 × 7.85 = 137.91 kg
Total weight: 137.91 + 6.15 = 144.06 kg/plate
Stainless Steel

S.S. Sheets & Plates

L(m) × W(m) × Thk(mm) × 8 = kg/sheet
L(ft) × W(m) × Thk(mm) × ¾ = kg/sheet
  • Metric: Length(m) × Width(m) × Thickness(mm) × 8.0
  • Imperial length: Length(ft) × Width(m) × Thickness(mm) × 0.75
  • Grades: AISI 304, 304L, 316, 316L, 321
Stainless Steel

S.S. Circles & Circular Blanks

Dia(mm)² × Thk(mm) ÷ 160 = gms/pc
Dia(mm)² × Thk(mm) × 0.00000628 = kg/pc
  • Dia: Outer diameter of circular blank in millimetres
  • Thk: Thickness in millimetres (mm)
  • Output: Direct weight in grams (÷ 160) or kilograms
Stainless Steel

S.S. Pipes & Tubes

(OD − WT) × WT × 0.0248 = kg/m
(OD − WT) × WT × 0.00758 = kg/ft
  • OD: Outside diameter in mm
  • WT: Wall thickness in mm
  • Units: Top formula in kg/metre, lower in kg/foot
Stainless Steel

S.S. Round Bars

Dia(mm)² × 0.00623 = kg/m
Dia(mm)² × 0.0019 = kg/ft
  • Dia: Nominal diameter in millimetres (mm)
  • Per metre: Dia² × 0.00623 kg/m (or d² ÷ 160.5)
  • Per foot: Dia² × 0.0019 kg/ft
Stainless Steel

S.S. Square Bars

Side(mm)² × 0.00788 = kg/m
Side(mm)² × 0.0024 = kg/ft
  • Side: Width / side dimension in millimetres (mm)
  • Per metre: Side² × 0.00788 kg/m
  • Per foot: Side² × 0.0024 kg/ft
Stainless Steel

S.S. Hexagonal Bars

Across-Flats(mm)² × 0.00680 = kg/m
Across-Flats(mm)² × 0.002072 = kg/ft
  • Across-Flats (A/F): Width across opposing flat faces in mm
  • Per metre: A/F² × 0.00680 kg/m
  • Per foot: A/F² × 0.002072 kg/ft
Stainless Steel

S.S. Flat Bars

W(mm) × Thk(mm) × 0.00798 = kg/m
W(mm) × Thk(mm) × 0.00243 = kg/ft
  • W: Width of flat bar in millimetres (mm)
  • Thk: Thickness of flat bar in millimetres (mm)
  • Units: Top in kg/metre, lower in kg/foot
Non-Ferrous

Copper Pipes & Tubes

(OD − WT) × WT × 0.0256 = kg/m
  • OD: Outside diameter in millimetres (mm)
  • WT: Wall thickness in millimetres (mm)
  • Density: Pure copper ~8,940 kg/m³
Non-Ferrous

Brass Pipes & Tubes

(OD − WT) × WT × 0.0260 = kg/m
  • OD: Outside diameter in millimetres (mm)
  • WT: Wall thickness in millimetres (mm)
  • Application: Commercial brass tubing & condenser pipes
Non-Ferrous

Aluminium Pipes & Tubes

(OD − WT) × WT × 0.0083 = kg/m
  • OD: Outside diameter in millimetres (mm)
  • WT: Wall thickness in millimetres (mm)
  • Density factor: 0.0082 to 0.0083 (~2,700 kg/m³)
Non-Ferrous

Aluminium Sheets & Plates

L(m) × W(m) × Thk(mm) × 2.69 = kg/sheet
  • L: Length in metres (m) · W: Width in metres (m)
  • Thk: Thickness in millimetres (mm)
  • Basis: Commercial aluminium density ~2.69 kg/m² per mm
Non-Ferrous

Lead Pipes & Sleeves (approx)

(OD − WT) × WT × 0.0345 = kg/m
  • OD: Outside diameter in millimetres (mm)
  • WT: Wall thickness in millimetres (mm)
  • Application: Radiation shielding, chemical plumbing
Non-Ferrous

Lead Sheets (approx)

L(m) × W(m) × Thk(mm) × 11.2 = kg/sheet
  • L: Length in metres (m) · W: Width in metres (m)
  • Thk: Thickness in millimetres (mm)
  • Density: ~11,340 kg/m³ (1 mm sheet ~ 11.2 kg/m²)
Engineering Units

Tensile Strength & Pressure Equivalents

1 kg/mm² × 9.81 = 1 N/mm² = 1 MPa
1 PSI × 0.000703 = 1 kg/mm²
  • 1 KSI: 1,000 PSI = 6.895 MPa
  • 1 kg/mm²: 1.422 KSI = 1,422 PSI
  • Metric to Imperial length: 1 Metre = 3.2808 Feet
  • Weight per foot: Weight per metre ÷ 3.2808
Pressure Design

Barlow’s Formula for Calculating Internal Bursting Pressure of Pipe / Tube

Barlow’s formula relates internal fluid pressure to allowable pipe wall dimensions and material tensile strength:

P = (2 · S · t) ÷ D
Wall Thickness:
t = (P · D) ÷ (2 · S)
Fiber Stress:
S = (P · D) ÷ (2 · t)
  • P: Internal Bursting or Working Pressure (PSI or N/mm² / bar)
  • S: Allowable Tensile / Yield Strength of tube material (PSI or N/mm²)
  • t: Wall Thickness in inches or millimetres
  • D: Outside Diameter of pipe/tube in inches or millimetres

Safety Note: A suitable safety factor (typically 3:1 to 4:1 for working pressure) must always be applied to calculate maximum allowable working pressure (MAWP) from theoretical bursting pressure.
